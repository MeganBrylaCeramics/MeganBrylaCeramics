Where to find clay in New York

A live map of New York State with places linked to natural clay deposits. The base map and geology layers are loaded live from public servers. The markers are my own list.

Before you dig: every spot here is approximate. Most land is private, and parks, preserves and state land usually forbid removing soil or rock. Get the landowner's permission first, and never dig on a bluff or stream bank that could collapse.

New York clay soil map

USDA soil survey clay data, every stream and lake, 124 documented USGS clay pits and day-by-day digging conditions. Zoom in to see the soil colored by how much clay it holds, with streams that cut through clay drawn in orange.

Before you dig: most land is private, active pits are commercial property, and state land forbids removing soil without a permit. Ask the landowner first, and never dig into a bank that could collapse.

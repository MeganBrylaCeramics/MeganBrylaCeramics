New York clay soil map

Pick a date and the map shows which clay areas are workable that day, using live soil temperature, soil moisture, snow and rain. Tap any spot in the state for its USDA soil survey data.

Before you dig: every spot is approximate. Most land is private, and parks, preserves and state land usually forbid removing soil or rock. Get the landowner's permission first, and never dig on a bluff or stream bank that could collapse. Conditions are a rough guide from modeled weather data, not a field check.

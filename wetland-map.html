        Reading wetland codes

        Each inventory wetland has a code like PFO1E. The first letter is the system, the next two the class, and the last letter the water regime. Tap a wetland and the popup spells it out.

        P
        Palustrine: marshes, swamps, bogs, ponds
        L
        Lacustrine: lakes
        R
        Riverine: rivers and streams
        E
        Estuarine: tidal, brackish water
        EM
        Emergent: cattails, sedges, grasses
        SS
        Scrub-shrub: alder, willow, dogwood
        FO
        Forested: red maple, ash, hemlock swamps
        UB
        Open water bottom
        Rules and sources

        Maps are a guide, not a legal boundary. Since January 1, 2025, NYS DEC protects freshwater wetlands larger than 12.4 acres whether or not they are on its maps (the old maps are now called "previously mapped" wetlands), plus smaller wetlands of unusual importance. The size threshold drops to 7.4 acres in 2028. Work in a protected wetland or the area around it, usually 100 feet, generally needs a permit, so contact DEC first.

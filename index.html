FAQ

How does shipping work?

Can two people buy the same piece?

No. Each piece is one of a kind, and the checkout link stops working once it sells. For Venmo pickups, I confirm the piece is still available before taking payment.

Are they food safe?

Yes, unless the listing says otherwise. All functional pieces use food-safe glazes and are fired to stoneware temperatures.

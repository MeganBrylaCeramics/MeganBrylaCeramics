About the artist

Megan Bryla

I'm a ceramic artist from the Greater Binghamton area of New York. I graduated from Hartwick College in 2026 with a triple major in Environment, Sustainability & Society, French, and Art, earning Departmental Distinction in all three, and my artwork was honored in juried art shows along the way.

I throw every piece by hand on the wheel, then trim, glaze and fire it. I love glazes that pool and break over the edges, loose brushed stripes, and speckled clay that shows where it came from. Alongside functional pieces like mugs and tumblers, I make illustrated work: cylinders drawn like comic panels, where everyday moments wrap around the pot.

When I'm not at the wheel, I work in environmental conservation. As a Watershed Technician with the Upper Susquehanna Coalition, I help restore wetlands and water quality in the Upper Susquehanna River Basin.

— Megan

        How it's made

        The process

        1. 01

          Throw

          Each piece starts as a ball of clay, centered and pulled up on the wheel.

        2. 02

          Trim

          Once leather-hard, it's flipped and trimmed to carve the foot and refine the shape.

        3. 03

          Bisque

          A first firing turns the clay into porous ceramic, ready for glaze.

        4. 04

          Glaze & fire

          Dipped, layered and fired again to around 2,200°F, where the glaze melts into glass.
